# 07 - Asset Generation Prompts

## Cutout subject (Move 1)
"Isolated [subject] on transparent background, clean edges, soft contact shadow, high resolution, no box or frame, studio-neutral lighting"

## Scene background (Move 2)
"Full-bleed atmospheric [mood] scene for a [app type] screen, soft depth, no text, no UI, suitable as a background with room for overlay content, cinematic lighting"

## Motif artwork (Move 3)
"[Theme] illustration suitable for album/artwork crop, strong central motif that can be echoed in UI controls, limited palette of 3-5 colours, flat or soft 3D"

Always generate higher resolution than the final display size and test the crop/mask.
