# 10 - Worked Examples

## Plant care (Move 1)
Boring rectangular photo → cutout plant, oversized, leaves overlapping large serif title. Stats in frosted bottom panel.

## Weather (Move 2)
Flat blue → full-bleed sky scene, oversized translucent temperature, frosted forecast strip and detail cards.

## Music player (Move 3)
Default circular art + standard controls → scalloped crop, curved title, palette-matched and motif-matched buttons + wavy scrubber.

## Additional patterns
- Fitness / workout: light Move 2 (state-driven scene) + restrained Move 3 on controls.
- Banking / data-dense: usually **do not** apply heavy Move 1 or 2; light Move 3 (tokens, radii, type) only.
- Food / recipe detail: strong Move 1 on the dish photo.
- Podcast / long-form: Move 3 on artwork + episode title treatment.
