## What the source is

- A ~17-second vertical (720x1280) short-form video, creator handle **@ayoships** (shown as an Instagram watermark).
- Format: a presenter speaking to camera with word-by-word burned-in captions. Phone mockups pop in at the top of the frame showing **before (left)** and **after (right)** versions of an app screen as each tip is spoken.
- Topic: how to fix app UI that looks boring, flat, or generic, in three quick moves. The mockups look like AI-generated or AI-assisted app designs; the tips work as design direction you can give to a designer or an AI tool, or apply yourself.
- The audio track could not be transcribed directly in the environment this skill was built in (no speech-to-text available), so the script below is reconstructed from the burned-in captions by OCR and frame inspection. Treat small wording differences as possible; the three techniques and the three examples are unambiguous.

## Reconstructed script

> "If your layout feels boring, pull out the main subject, make it oversized, and overlap the headline.
> If the screen feels flat, make your background a scene, then layer your content on top.
> If it feels generic, try scalloped image crops, curved lettering, and buttons that match the style."

## The three before/after cases

### Case 1 - Plant detail screen ("layout feels boring")
Subject cut out, oversized, overlapping large serif headline. Secondary info in calm lower region.

### Case 2 - Weather app ("screen feels flat")
Full-bleed scene background, oversized translucent temperature, frosted panels for data.

### Case 3 - Music player ("it feels generic")
Scalloped album crop, curved title lettering, controls matching the artwork palette and motif.

The source gives the *what*. This skill adds diagnosis framework, exact geometry/values, cross-platform recipes, asset prompts, accessibility/performance guardrails, and more worked examples.
