Prefer tinted neutrals over pure grey/black/white. Choose the ramp that matches the world in the Direction card.

## Accent
- Keep a real brand color when one exists.
- Without a brand color, derive from the world (warm → amber/coral; cool → cobalt/teal; natural → moss/sage).
- Avoid purple→blue family unless the brand actually is purple.
- One accent used with restraint. Everything highlighted = nothing stands out.

## Semantic
Positive and negative colors should sit comfortably inside the chosen ramp (not pure neon).
